Quick equivalents

    Values follow the course conversion sheet (rounded). Exact definitions differ slightly in the last digits, for example 1 cal = 4.184 J and 1 ft·lbf = 1.3558 J. In exercises use g ≈ 10 m/s² ≈ 32.2 ft/s² (standard gravity: 9.80665 m/s² = 32.174 ft/s²).
