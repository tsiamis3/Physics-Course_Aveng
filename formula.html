Constants

  • g = 32.2 ft/s² (SI: 9.81 m/s²; standard 32.174 ft/s²)
  • Water: 62.4 lb/ft³ (1000 kg/m³) · 1 atm = 14.7 psi (101 325 Pa)
  • 1 hp = 550 ft·lb/s (745.7 W) · 1 BTU ≈ 778 ft·lb · 1 slug = 32.2 lb (mass)

Kinematics

  • Velocity: v = x/t · uniform motion: v = const, x = v·t
  • Uniform acceleration: v = v₀ + a·t
  • x = x₀ + v₀·t + ½·a·t²
  • v² = v₀² + 2·a·Δx
  • Free fall: a = g = 32.2 ft/s²

Newton’s Laws & Forces

  • 1st law (inertia): ΣF = 0 ⇔ constant velocity
  • 2nd law: ΣF = m·a, with m = W/g (m in slugs, F in lb, a in ft/s²)
  • 3rd law, action–reaction: equal in size, opposite in direction
  • Weight: W = m·g

Friction

  • Static friction: equal and opposite to the applied force, up to its maximum, the limiting friction
  • Sliding (kinetic) friction: F_f = μ·N (a little smaller than the limiting friction; μ = friction coefficient, N = normal force)
  • On a level surface N = W; on a slope N = W·cos θ

Work, Power & Energy

  • Work: W = F·x (force along the displacement x); in general W = F·x·cos θ [ft·lb]
  • Kinetic energy: KE = ½·m·v² = ½·(W/g)·v²
  • Potential energy (gravitational): PE = m·g·h = W·h
  • Conservation of mechanical energy: E = KE + PE = const (no friction or heat losses)
  • Power: P = Work/t = F·v [ft·lb/s; ÷ 550 for hp; SI: W = J/s]

Momentum & Collisions

  • p = m·v
  • Impulse: F·Δt = Δp
  • Collisions: total momentum is conserved; kinetic energy only if elastic

Torque & Equilibrium

  • Torque: τ = F·d (d = perpendicular distance, the lever arm) [lb·ft or lb·in; N·m]
  • Equilibrium of a rigid body: ΣF = 0 and Στ = 0
  • Work by a torque: W = τ·θ (θ in rad)
  • Shaft power: P = τ·ω
  • Engine: hp = τ [lb·ft] × n [rpm] ÷ 5252, so τ = hp × 5252 ÷ rpm. SI: P [kW] = τ [N·m] × n [rpm] ÷ 9549
  • At constant power, higher rpm means lower torque
  • Do not mix lb·ft (torque) with ft·lb (work), and rpm with rad/s

Circular & Rotational Motion

  • ω = 2π·n/60 (n in rpm, ω in rad/s)
  • v = ω·r
  • a_c = v²/r
  • Centripetal force: F_c = m·v²/r = (W/g)·v²/r, always perpendicular to v, pointing to the centre
  • Centrifugal force: a fictitious force, needed only in the rotating frame

Oscillations & Waves

  • T = 1/f
  • v = f·λ
  • Spring: F = k·x · T = 2π·√(m/k)
  • Pendulum (small oscillations): T = 2π·√(L/g); depends on length, not on mass
  • Periodic motion: period T, frequency f, amplitude

Stress

  • σ = F/A (stress), units of pressure: psi (Pa = N/m²), but not the same quantity as pressure
  • Types: tensile, compressive, shear

Simple Machines

  • A machine trades force for distance (or speed); it does not create work or energy
  • Mechanical advantage: MA = F_load / F_effort
  • Velocity ratio: VR = distance moved by the effort / distance moved by the load
  • Ideal machine: MA = VR · real machine: efficiency = MA/VR = W_out/W_in
  • Lever: F_e·L_e = F_l·L_l, ideal MA = L_e/L_l (effort arm / load arm)
  • 1st class: fulcrum between effort and load (seesaw, crowbar, pliers)
  • 2nd class: load between fulcrum and effort (wheelbarrow, nutcracker), MA > 1
  • 3rd class: effort between fulcrum and load (tweezers, forearm), MA < 1

Fluids & Pressure

  • P = F/A [psi = lb/in²]
  • Static pressure of a liquid: P = ρ·g·h (depends only on depth and density); with the atmosphere P = P₀ + ρ·g·h = P₀ + γ·h (γ = weight density, water 62.4 lb/ft³)
  • Ram (dynamic) pressure: P = ½·ρ·v²
  • Buoyancy (Archimedes): F_b = ρ_fluid·g·V_displaced = γ_fluid·V_displaced (weight of the displaced fluid)

Matter & Atomic Structure

  • A = Z + N (mass number = protons + neutrons) · N = A − Z
  • Neutral atom: number of electrons = Z; an ion has gained or lost electrons
  • Notation ᴬ_Z X, e.g. ²⁷₁₃Al: 13 protons, 14 neutrons, 13 electrons
  • Isotopes: same Z, different N
  • Mass density ρ = m/V · weight density γ = W/V
  • Specific gravity SG = ρ/ρ_water

Thermodynamics & Heat

  • Q = m·c·ΔT (water: c ≈ 1 BTU/(lb·°F))
  • Gas law: P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂ (T absolute)
  • °R = °F + 459.67 · K = °C + 273.15 · °F = °C × 9/5 + 32
