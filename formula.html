Constants

  • g = 32.2 ft/s² (SI: 9.81 m/s²; standard 32.174 ft/s²)
  • Water: 62.4 lb/ft³ (1000 kg/m³) = 1.94 slug/ft³
  • Air at sea level: ρ = 0.002377 slug/ft³
  • 1 atm = 14.7 psi (101 325 Pa) · 1 ft² = 144 in²
  • 1 hp = 550 ft·lb/s (745.7 W) · 1 BTU ≈ 778 ft·lb
  • 1 slug has a weight of 32.2 lb (mass in slugs = W/32.2)
  • 1 US gallon = 0.1337 ft³ · 1 knot = 1.688 ft/s · 1 mph = 1.467 ft/s

Kinematics

  • Velocity: v = x/t
  • Uniform motion: v = const, x = v·t
  • Uniform acceleration: v = v₀ + a·t
  • x = x₀ + v₀·t + ½·a·t²
  • v² = v₀² + 2·a·Δx
  • Free fall: a = g = 32.2 ft/s²

Newton’s Laws & Forces

  • 1st law (inertia): ΣF = 0 ⇔ constant velocity
  • 2nd law: ΣF = m·a, with m = W/g (m in slugs, F in lb, a in ft/s²)
  • 3rd law, action–reaction: equal in size, opposite in direction
  • Weight: W = m·g

Friction

  • Static friction: equal and opposite to the applied force, up to its maximum, the limiting friction
  • Starting (limiting) friction: F = k·N
  • Sliding (kinetic) friction: F_f = μ·N (a little smaller than the limiting friction; μ = friction coefficient, N = normal force)
  • On a level surface N = W; on a slope N = W·cos θ
  • Rolling friction (wheels, rollers) is much smaller than sliding friction

Work, Power & Energy

  • Work: W = F·x (force along the displacement x); in general W = F·x·cos θ [ft·lb]
  • Kinetic energy: KE = ½·m·v² = ½·(W/g)·v²
  • Potential energy (gravitational): PE = m·g·h = W·h
  • Conservation of mechanical energy: E = KE + PE = const (no friction or heat losses)
  • Power: P = Work/t = F·v [ft·lb/s; ÷ 550 for hp; SI: W = J/s]
  • Efficiency = P_out/P_in = W_out/W_in (always below 100 %)

Momentum & Collisions

  • Momentum: p = m·v (a vector); m in slugs, p in slug·ft/s = lb·s
  • Impulse: F·Δt = Δp = m·Δv, so a longer stopping time means a smaller average force
  • Conservation: with no external force, m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ = total momentum before = after (sign the velocities)
  • Stick together: v = (m₁v₁ + m₂v₂)/(m₁ + m₂)
  • Total momentum is conserved in every collision; kinetic energy only if elastic
  • Jet thrust ≈ (mass flow of air) × Δv, in slug/s × ft/s = lb

Gyroscopes 21 Oct

  • Rigidity in space: a spinning free gyro keeps its direction; it grows with mass, spin speed, mass near the rim and low bearing friction (attitude and directional indicators)
  • Precession: the gyro reacts as if the force were applied 90° ahead in the direction of rotation (turn and bank)

Torque & Equilibrium

  • Torque: τ = F·d (d = perpendicular distance, the lever arm) [lb·ft or lb·in; N·m]
  • Torque wrench with an extension E on a wrench of length L: reading = applied torque × L/(L + E)
  • Equilibrium of a rigid body: ΣF = 0 and Στ = 0
  • Work by a torque: W = τ·θ (θ in rad)
  • Shaft power: P = τ·ω
  • Engine: hp = τ [lb·ft] × n [rpm] ÷ 5252, so τ = hp × 5252 ÷ rpm. SI: P [kW] = τ [N·m] × n [rpm] ÷ 9549
  • At constant power, higher rpm means lower torque
  • Do not mix lb·ft (torque) with ft·lb (work), and rpm with rad/s

Circular & Rotational Motion

  • ω = 2π·n/60 (n in rpm, ω in rad/s)
  • v = ω·r
  • a_c = v²/r
  • Centripetal force: F_c = m·v²/r = (W/g)·v²/r, always perpendicular to v, pointing to the centre
  • Centrifugal force: a fictitious force, needed only in the rotating frame

Oscillations & Waves

  • T = 1/f
  • v = f·λ
  • Spring: F = k·x
  • Mass on a spring: T = 2π·√(m/k)
  • Pendulum (small oscillations): T = 2π·√(L/g); depends on length, not on mass
  • Periodic motion: period T, frequency f, amplitude

Stress

  • σ = F/A (stress), units of pressure: psi (Pa = N/m²), but not the same quantity as pressure
  • Types: tension, compression, shear, torsion (twisting), bending (tension on one side, compression on the other)
  • Elasticity: the material returns to its shape when the load is removed

Simple Machines

  • A machine trades force for distance (or speed); it does not create work or energy
  • Mechanical advantage: MA = F_load / F_effort
  • Velocity ratio: VR = distance moved by the effort / distance moved by the load
  • Ideal machine: MA = VR
  • Real machine: efficiency = MA/VR = W_out/W_in
  • Lever: F_e·L_e = F_l·L_l, ideal MA = L_e/L_l (effort arm / load arm)
  • 1st class: fulcrum between effort and load (seesaw, crowbar, pliers)
  • 2nd class: load between fulcrum and effort (wheelbarrow, nutcracker), MA > 1
  • 3rd class: effort between fulcrum and load (tweezers, forearm, landing gear), MA < 1
  • Pulleys: fixed MA = 1; movable MA = 2; block and tackle ideal MA = number of rope segments supporting the load
  • Gears: ratio = N_driven/N_drive = n_drive/n_driven; ideal torque out = torque in × ratio
  • Inclined plane: ideal MA = length/height
  • Wedge: ideal MA = length/thickness · Screw: ideal MA = 2π·r/pitch

Fluids & Pressure

  • P = F/A [psi = lb/in²]
  • Static pressure of a liquid: P = ρ·g·h (depends only on depth and density; ρ in slug/ft³ gives lb/ft²); with the atmosphere P = P₀ + ρ·g·h = P₀ + γ·h (γ = weight density, water 62.4 lb/ft³ ≈ 0.433 psi per ft of depth)
  • Dynamic pressure (ram): q = ½·ρ·v²
  • Buoyancy (Archimedes): F_b = ρ_fluid·g·V_displaced = γ_fluid·V_displaced (weight of the displaced fluid)

Pascal & Hydraulics 22 Oct

  • Pascal: pressure in a confined fluid acts equally in all directions, at right angles to the walls
  • F = P·A, P = F/A, A = F/P (lb, psi, in²)
  • Hydraulic MA = F_out/F_in = A_out/A_in = d_in/d_out
  • Volume in = volume out: A_in·d_in = A_out·d_out (we gain force, lose distance; no gain in work)
  • Piston area A = π·d²/4

Fluid Properties & Flow 23 Oct

  • Density: ρ = m/V · weight density γ = W/V (water 62.4 lb/ft³)
  • Specific gravity SG = ρ/ρ_water (no units); hydrometer reading 1.275 = SG 1.275
  • Viscosity: resistance to flow; for liquids it increases when the temperature falls
  • Compressibility: liquids are almost incompressible, gases are compressible
  • Continuity: A₁·v₁ = A₂·v₂ (a narrower duct means a faster flow)
  • Bernoulli: where v increases, static pressure decreases
  • Total pressure = static pressure + dynamic pressure (q = ½·ρ·v²)
  • Pitot-static: q = P_total − P_static, v = √(2q/ρ)

Matter & Atomic Structure

  • A = Z + N (mass number = protons + neutrons), N = A − Z
  • Neutral atom: number of electrons = Z; an ion has gained or lost electrons
  • Notation AZX, e.g. 2713Al: 13 protons, 14 neutrons, 13 electrons
  • Isotopes: same Z, different N
  • Mass density ρ = m/V · weight density γ = W/V
  • Specific gravity SG = ρ/ρ_water

Thermodynamics & Heat

  • Q = m·c·ΔT (water: c ≈ 1 BTU/(lb·°F))
  • Gas law: P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂ (T absolute)
  • °R = °F + 459.67
  • K = °C + 273.15
  • °F = °C × 9/5 + 32
