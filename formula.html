Constants

  • g = 32.2 ft/s² (SI: 9.81 m/s²; standard 32.174 ft/s²)
  • Water: 62.4 lb/ft³ (1000 kg/m³) · 1 atm = 14.7 psi (101 325 Pa)
  • 1 hp = 550 ft·lb/s · 1 BTU ≈ 778 ft·lb · 1 slug = 32.2 lb (mass)

Kinematics

  • v = v₀ + a·t
  • x = x₀ + v₀·t + ½·a·t²
  • v² = v₀² + 2·a·Δx
  • Free fall: a = g = 32.2 ft/s²

Newton’s Laws & Forces

  • ΣF = m·a, with m = W/g (m in slugs, F in lb, a in ft/s²)
  • Weight: W = m·g
  • Action–reaction: equal in size, opposite in direction

Friction

  • f = μ·N (μ = friction coefficient, N = normal force)
  • On a level surface N = W; on a slope N = W·cos θ

Work, Power & Energy

  • Work: W = F·d·cos θ [ft·lb]
  • Kinetic energy: KE = ½·m·v² = ½·(W/g)·v²
  • Potential energy: PE = W·h
  • Power: P = Work/t = F·v [ft·lb/s; ÷ 550 for hp]

Momentum & Collisions

  • p = m·v
  • Impulse: F·Δt = Δp
  • Collisions: total momentum is conserved; kinetic energy only if elastic

Torque & Equilibrium

  • τ = F·d (d perpendicular to the force) [lb·ft or lb·in]
  • Equilibrium: ΣF = 0 and Στ = 0
  • Engine: torque [lb·ft] = hp × 5252 ÷ rpm

Circular & Rotational Motion

  • ω = 2π·n/60 (n in rpm, ω in rad/s)
  • v = ω·r
  • a_c = v²/r
  • F_c = m·v²/r = (W/g)·v²/r

Oscillations & Waves

  • T = 1/f
  • v = f·λ
  • Spring: F = k·x · T = 2π·√(m/k)
  • Pendulum: T = 2π·√(L/g)

Fluids & Pressure

  • P = F/A [psi = lb/in²]
  • Pressure at depth: P = P₀ + γ·h (γ = weight density, water 62.4 lb/ft³)
  • Buoyancy: F_b = γ_fluid · V_displaced

Matter & Density

  • Mass density ρ = m/V · weight density γ = W/V
  • Specific gravity SG = ρ/ρ_water

Thermodynamics & Heat

  • Q = m·c·ΔT (water: c ≈ 1 BTU/(lb·°F))
  • Gas law: P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂ (T absolute)
  • °R = °F + 459.67 · K = °C + 273.15 · °F = °C × 9/5 + 32
