Answers and explanations

1. A gas differs from a liquid in that a gas: 2.1

  • a) cannot flow
  • b) has a fixed volume
  • c) fills the whole of its container and can be compressed ✔
Why: Gas particles are far apart and move freely, so a gas spreads out to fill its container and can be squeezed. A liquid has a fixed volume.

2. In a hydrogen molecule (H₂), the two atoms are held together by: 2.1

  • a) sharing their electrons (a covalent bond) ✔
  • b) sharing their protons
  • c) magnetic attraction
Why: A covalent bond is a shared pair of electrons. Protons stay in the nucleus and are never shared.

3. A liquid that evaporates readily at room temperature is described as: 2.1

  • a) inert
  • b) volatile ✔
  • c) viscous
Why: Volatile = evaporates easily (e.g. fuel). Viscous means thick; inert means it does not react.

4. The hardener of a two-part epoxy makes the resin cure faster. It acts as a: 2.1

  • a) catalyst ✔
  • b) inhibitor
  • c) solvent
Why: A catalyst speeds up a chemical reaction. An inhibitor slows it down.

5. A small insect can stand on the surface of still water because of: 2.1

  • a) viscosity
  • b) buoyancy
  • c) surface tension ✔
Why: The surface molecules are pulled inwards and act like a stretched skin. The insect does not sink, so it is not buoyancy.

6. Within the elastic limit, the extension of a spring is: 2.2.1

  • a) independent of the load
  • b) proportional to the square of the load
  • c) proportional to the load ✔
Why: Hooke's law.

7. A beam fixed at one end and loaded at its free end (a cantilever) has its upper surface in: 2.2.1

  • a) pure shear
  • b) tension ✔
  • c) compression
Why: It bends downwards: top stretched, bottom squeezed.

8. As an aircraft climbs, the aneroid capsule of its altimeter: 2.2.1

  • a) expands, because the outside pressure falls ✔
  • b) contracts, because the outside pressure falls
  • c) does not change
Why: The capsule is sealed. As the outside pressure falls with altitude, it expands, and this drives the pointer.

9. In a hydraulic press, a force of 20 lb on a 1 in² piston acts through the fluid on a 15 in² piston. The output force is: 2.2.1

  • a) 1.33 lb
  • b) 35 lb
  • c) 300 lb ✔
Why: Same pressure (20 psi) × 15 in².

10. To find the CG of an aircraft, the total moment is divided by the: 2.2.1

  • a) total weight ✔
  • b) distance from the datum
  • c) number of wheels
Why: CG = total moment ÷ total weight. Moments are weight × distance, so dividing by weight gives a distance (the arm).

11. A pressure of 1 pascal is: 2.2.1

  • a) 1 N acting on 1 m² ✔
  • b) 1 N acting on 1 cm²
  • c) 1 lb acting on 1 in²
Why: Pa = N/m². 1 lb on 1 in² is 1 psi.

12. A 5 000 lb boat floats in fresh water. The weight of the water it displaces is: 2.2.1

  • a) 5 000 lb ✔
  • b) less than 5 000 lb
  • c) more than 5 000 lb
Why: Archimedes: a floating body displaces its own weight of fluid.

13. The third harmonic of a 40 Hz fundamental is: 2.2.2

  • a) 13.3 Hz
  • b) 43 Hz
  • c) 120 Hz ✔
Why: 3 × 40.

14. A wheelbarrow is a lever of the: 2.2.2

  • a) third class: the effort is between the fulcrum and the load
  • b) second class: the load is between the fulcrum and the effort ✔
  • c) first class: the fulcrum is between the load and the effort
Why: Class 2: load in the middle (wheel = fulcrum, handles = effort). It always gives MA > 1.

15. A 15-tooth gear drives a 45-tooth gear. Compared with the driving gear, the driven gear turns: 2.2.2

  • a) three times slower, with three times the torque ✔
  • b) at the same speed and torque
  • c) three times faster, with a third of the torque
Why: Gear ratio = 45/15 = 3. The large driven gear turns 3 times slower but gives 3 times the torque.

16. A vehicle starts from rest and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 10 s. The distance travelled is: 2.2.2

  • a) 20 m
  • b) 100 m ✔
  • c) 200 m
Why: x = ½at².

17. An aircraft turns at the same speed but on twice the radius. The centripetal force needed is: 2.2.2

  • a) four times greater
  • b) doubled
  • c) halved ✔
Why: F ∝ 1/r.

18. An 800 lb load is pushed up a ramp 20 ft long and 5 ft high. The ideal effort is: 2.2.2

  • a) 160 lb
  • b) 200 lb ✔
  • c) 3 200 lb
Why: MA = 20/5 = 4.

19. A screw jack's handle moves 20 in per turn and its thread pitch is 0.1 in. Its ideal mechanical advantage is: 2.2.2

  • a) 0.005
  • b) 2
  • c) 200 ✔
Why: MA = distance per turn / pitch.

20. A 10 kg toolbox is taken to the Moon, where g ≈ 1.6 m/s². Its mass there is: 2.2.3a

  • a) 1.6 kg
  • b) 10 kg ✔
  • c) 16 kg
Why: Mass does not change; only the weight does.

21. A rifle recoils backwards when it fires a bullet forwards. This illustrates Newton's: 2.2.3a

  • a) law of gravitation
  • b) first law
  • c) third law ✔
Why: Action and reaction: the gun pushes the bullet forwards and the bullet pushes the gun backwards with an equal force.

22. A pendulum swings from its highest point to its lowest point. At the lowest point: 2.2.3a

  • a) its kinetic energy is zero
  • b) its kinetic energy is greatest and its potential energy least ✔
  • c) its total energy is greatest
Why: Total energy stays the same. At the bottom height is lowest (PE least), so speed and KE are greatest.

23. An aircraft of 200 slug (6 440 lb) taxis at 20 ft/s. Its kinetic energy is: 2.2.3a

  • a) 40 000 ft·lb ✔
  • b) 80 000 ft·lb
  • c) 1 288 000 ft·lb
Why: ½ × 200 × 20². Traps: forgetting ½, or using the weight.

24. Torque (lb·ft) and work (ft·lb) have similar units, but: 2.2.3a

  • a) they are the same quantity
  • b) torque can exist without movement, work cannot ✔
  • c) work can exist without movement, torque cannot
Why: Torque = force × arm, even if nothing turns (a tight bolt). Work needs movement: force × distance moved.

25. The attitude indicator (artificial horizon) works on the gyroscopic property of: 2.2.3b

  • a) precession
  • b) rigidity in space ✔
  • c) centrifugal force
Why: A spinning gyro keeps its axis fixed in space, so the horizon stays level while the aircraft pitches and rolls around it.

26. A gyroscope in one gimbal, free to turn about only one axis at right angles to its spin axis, is a: 2.2.3b

  • a) captive (rate) gyro ✔
  • b) fixed rotor
  • c) free gyro
Why: One gimbal = captive (rate) gyro, e.g. the turn indicator. A free gyro has two gimbals.

27. A force of 30 lb is needed to slide a 100 lb crate. The coefficient of sliding friction is: 2.2.3b

  • a) 0.3 ✔
  • b) 3.33
  • c) 3 000
Why: μ = F/N.

28. A car of 1 500 kg moves at 20 m/s. Its momentum is: 2.2.3b

  • a) 75 kg·m/s
  • b) 30 000 kg·m/s ✔
  • c) 300 000 kg·m/s
Why: p = mv. Trap: ½mv² gives 300 000.

29. The density of fresh water in imperial units is about: 2.2.4a

  • a) 8.34 lb/ft³
  • b) 62.4 lb/ft³ ✔
  • c) 1 lb/ft³
Why: 8.34 is lb per US gallon.

30. A gas with a specific gravity of 0.07 relative to air is: 2.2.4a

  • a) much lighter than air ✔
  • b) much heavier than air
  • c) as dense as water
Why: Hydrogen.

31. Fairings and smooth rounded shapes are fitted to aircraft parts in order to: 2.2.4b

  • a) increase the static pressure on them
  • b) reduce drag (fluid resistance) ✔
  • c) increase the lift of the parts
Why: Smooth, streamlined shapes reduce drag. They do not add lift.

32. If the airspeed is tripled, the dynamic pressure is multiplied by: 2.2.4b

  • a) 3
  • b) 6
  • c) 9 ✔
Why: q ∝ v².

33. An airspeed indicator works by comparing: 2.2.4b

  • a) two temperatures
  • b) two static pressures
  • c) the total (pitot) pressure with the static pressure ✔
Why: Dynamic pressure = pitot (total) − static. The ASI shows this difference as speed.

34. The cylinder head temperature (CHT) of a piston engine is normally measured with: 2.3a

  • a) an iron– or copper–constantan thermocouple ✔
  • b) a bimetallic strip
  • c) a mercury thermometer
Why: CHT is a high temperature, so a thermocouple is used (iron–constantan or copper–constantan).

35. Compare a cup of boiling water with a bath of warm water. Which is correct? 2.3a

  • a) the cup is at a higher temperature, but the bath holds more heat energy ✔
  • b) the cup holds more heat energy
  • c) both hold the same heat energy
Why: Temperature shows how hot something is; heat energy also depends on the mass. The bath has much more mass.

36. A gas occupies 5 ft³ at 500 °R. At constant pressure it is cooled to 400 °R. Its new volume is: 2.3b

  • a) 4 ft³ ✔
  • b) 5 ft³
  • c) 6.25 ft³
Why: Charles: V ∝ T.

37. The volume of a gas is halved while its absolute temperature is doubled. Its absolute pressure becomes: 2.3b

  • a) twice as great
  • b) four times greater ✔
  • c) unchanged
Why: PV/T = constant.

38. An aluminium bar 2 m long (α = 23 × 10⁻⁶ per °C) is heated by 100 °C. It expands by: 2.3b

  • a) 0.46 mm
  • b) 4.6 mm ✔
  • c) 46 mm
Why: ΔL = α·L·ΔT.

39. A heat pump heating a cabin: 2.3b

  • a) moves heat from the cold outside air into the warmer cabin, using work ✔
  • b) moves heat out of the cabin without work
  • c) creates heat without using energy
Why: A heat pump pumps heat from cold to hot. Second law: this needs work input.

40. The heat given out when water freezes into ice at 0 °C is the latent heat of: 2.3b

  • a) vaporisation
  • b) fusion ✔
  • c) sublimation
Why: Liquid ↔ solid is fusion. Vaporisation is liquid ↔ gas.

41. Avgas gives about 18 900 BTU per lb when burned. This value is its: 2.3b

  • a) heat of combustion ✔
  • b) latent heat
  • c) specific heat
Why: Heat of combustion = heat released per unit mass of fuel burned.

42. Raising the compression ratio of a piston engine increases its efficiency, but also the risk of: 2.3b

  • a) vapour lock
  • b) carburettor icing
  • c) detonation ✔
Why: A higher compression ratio gives higher temperature and pressure, so the mixture may explode instead of burning smoothly (detonation).

43. In an isothermal process, if the volume of a gas is halved, its pressure: 2.3b

  • a) halves
  • b) stays the same
  • c) doubles ✔
Why: PV = constant.

44. Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes to reach the Earth because its speed is about: 2.4

  • a) 300 km/s
  • b) 343 m/s
  • c) 300 000 km/s ✔
Why: c ≈ 3 × 10⁸ m/s = 300 000 km/s. 343 m/s is the speed of sound.

45. A make-up mirror that gives a magnified image is a: 2.4

  • a) plane mirror
  • b) convex mirror
  • c) concave mirror ✔
Why: A concave mirror magnifies when the face is close (inside its focal length). A convex mirror makes images smaller.

46. A diverging (concave) lens: 2.4

  • a) reflects the light back
  • b) spreads parallel rays apart ✔
  • c) brings parallel rays to a focus
Why: A concave lens is thin in the middle and spreads rays apart. A convex lens focuses them.

47. Inside glass, light meets the surface at less than the critical angle. The light: 2.4

  • a) stops at the surface
  • b) partly refracts out into the air ✔
  • c) is totally reflected
Why: Total internal reflection happens only above the critical angle. Below it, part of the light refracts out.

48. Joining two optical fibres by melting their ends together with an electric arc is called: 2.4

  • a) mechanical splicing
  • b) crimping
  • c) fusion splicing ✔
Why: Fusion splice = ends melted together by an electric arc. A mechanical splice aligns the ends in a clamp.

49. A cork on water bobs up and down as waves pass but does not move along with them. This shows that waves carry: 2.5

  • a) the water along with them
  • b) neither energy nor water
  • c) energy, not the water itself ✔
Why: A wave carries energy. The medium only oscillates about its position.

50. Light and radio waves are: 2.5

  • a) mechanical waves that need a medium
  • b) transverse waves that can travel through a vacuum ✔
  • c) longitudinal waves
Why: Electromagnetic waves are transverse and need no medium. Sound is longitudinal and needs a medium.

51. Two identical sound waves arrive exactly in phase. The result is: 2.5

  • a) a louder sound (constructive interference) ✔
  • b) silence
  • c) a lower pitch
Why: Crests meet crests, so the amplitudes add up and the sound is louder.

52. On a cold day, the speed of sound in air is ___ than on a hot day. 2.5

  • a) lower ✔
  • b) the same
  • c) higher
Why: The speed of sound depends on air temperature: colder air means lower speed.
